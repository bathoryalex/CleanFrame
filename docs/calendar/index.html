Show your calendar on Frame Home

Frame Home reads your calendar through its secret iCal address. The address is encrypted on this phone, so only your TV can read it.

1Copy the secret iCal address

Google Calendar (in a browser): Settings → choose your calendar under “Settings for my calendars” → Integrate calendar → Secret address in iCal format.

Outlook (outlook.com or Microsoft 365 on the web): Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar → choose the calendar and “Can view all details” → Publish → copy the ICS link.

On a phone the Google Calendar and Outlook apps do not show this address. Open calendar.google.com or outlook.com in the browser and switch to the desktop site (Chrome: ⋮ → Desktop site; Safari: aA → Request Desktop Website).

Treat it like a password: anyone with the address can see your events. You can reset it in the same place.

2Paste it and send it to the TV

Open this page by scanning the QR code on the TV.
